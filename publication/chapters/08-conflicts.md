# 8. Conflict rules

| ID | IF (condition) | THEN (action) | BECAUSE (reason) | Example (software/infra) | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <a id="k1"></a>K1 | a rule that adds parts ([P44](07-rules.md#p44), [P45](07-rules.md#p45), [P46](07-rules.md#p46), [P40](07-rules.md#p40), [P41](07-rules.md#p41), [P47](07-rules.md#p47)) conflicts with [P23](07-rules.md#p23) (fewer parts) | add those parts only as far as the master rule and the rigor matrix require | both adding and removing parts change failure risk | multi-AZ for production, single-AZ for development | sourced (see [P23](07-rules.md#p23), [P44](07-rules.md#p44), [P45](07-rules.md#p45)) |
| <a id="k2"></a>K2 | a fix is needed faster than [P64](07-rules.md#p64) (change review) and [P65](07-rules.md#p65) (small steps) allow, because of [P73](07-rules.md#p73) (time until harm) | use a predefined emergency change path with a shorter review, and do the full review afterwards | skipping review without a plan removes every check at the moment of highest stress | a hotfix path with one approver and a mandatory postmortem within 48 h | author's synthesis; no single source |
| <a id="k3"></a>K3 | [P27](07-rules.md#p27) (tolerate variation) seems to conflict with [P33](07-rules.md#p33) (reject bad input) | tolerate variation within the specification ([P27](07-rules.md#p27)); reject and report input outside the specification ([P33](07-rules.md#p33)) | both rules aim at stable behavior; they differ only in where the boundary lies | accept any valid timestamp format the schema allows; reject a timestamp field that is missing | author's synthesis from Taguchi and RFC 9413 |
| <a id="k4"></a>K4 | preserving evidence ([P83](07-rules.md#p83)) would delay recovery beyond the time until harm ([P73](07-rules.md#p73)) | capture evidence automatically and fast; if harm is imminent, restore service first and keep whatever the automation captured | both the evidence and the service matter, and automation makes the trade-off small | crash dumps and log shipping run before every automatic restart | author's synthesis; no single source |
| <a id="k5"></a>K5 | [P18](07-rules.md#p18) (decide reversible decisions fast) conflicts with [P64](07-rules.md#p64) (change review) and [P56](07-rules.md#p56) (independent checker) | scale the review depth with reversibility and consequence: reversible and low consequence → peer review or self-check; irreversible or high consequence → full review, [P42](07-rules.md#p42) and an external checker | both slowness and unreviewed irreversible harm are failures | a new dashboard: the team decides; deleting historical audit logs: two approvers and a legal review | author's synthesis from the Amazon 2015 letter and the master rule |
| <a id="k6"></a>K6 | [P7](07-rules.md#p7) (leave the method to the people doing the work) conflicts with [P71](07-rules.md#p71) (checklists), [P72](07-rules.md#p72) (critical control points) and [P61](07-rules.md#p61) (qualified procedure) | prescribe the method only at critical control points, in qualified or regulated procedures, and where the rigor matrix requires it; everywhere else give intent and constraints | prescribed methods everywhere stop initiative; free methods at critical points remove proven protections | "use the restore checklist" (prescribed); "improve developer onboarding" (intent only) | author's synthesis from ADP 6-0 and [P61](07-rules.md#p61) |
| <a id="k7"></a>K7 | [P5](07-rules.md#p5) (psychological safety) and [P80](07-rules.md#p80) (blameless analysis) seem to conflict with accountability ([P2](07-rules.md#p2), [P6](07-rules.md#p6), [P43](07-rules.md#p43), legal duties) | treat honest errors and system weaknesses without blame; treat deliberate violations, reckless behavior and fraud through a defined, fair disciplinary procedure | blame for honest errors hides information; no consequence for deliberate violations destroys trust and controls | a wrong command during an incident → blameless postmortem; bypassing the two-person rule on purpose → disciplinary procedure | author's synthesis; no single source |
| <a id="k8"></a>K8 | [P21](07-rules.md#p21) (specific, challenging goals) conflicts with [P19](07-rules.md#p19) (indicators are gamed) and with Deming's rejection of numerical goals | use goals for direction and learning; do not tie pay or ranking mechanically to one indicator; pair every target with a counter-indicator | goals raise performance, but a single rewarded number distorts the work | the team goal "halve build time" has a guard "test coverage does not drop"; bonuses are not calculated from the build time | author's synthesis from Locke & Latham, Campbell and Deming |

# 8. Conflict rules

| ID | IF (condition) | THEN (action) | BECAUSE (reason) | Example (software/infra) | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <a id="k1"></a>K1 | a rule that adds parts ([U25](07-rules.md#u25), [R1](07-rules.md#r1), [R2](07-rules.md#r2), [U29](07-rules.md#u29), [U34](07-rules.md#u34), [C3](07-rules.md#c3)) conflicts with [U7](07-rules.md#u7) (fewer parts) | add those parts only as far as the master rule and the rigor matrix require | both adding and removing parts change failure risk | multi-AZ for production, single-AZ for development | sourced (see [U7](07-rules.md#u7), [U25](07-rules.md#u25), [R1](07-rules.md#r1)) |
| <a id="k2"></a>K2 | a fix is needed faster than [U9](07-rules.md#u9) (change review) and [U19](07-rules.md#u19) (small steps) allow, because of [U35](07-rules.md#u35) (time until harm) | use a predefined emergency change path with a shorter review, and do the full review afterwards | skipping review without a plan removes every check at the moment of highest stress | a hotfix path with one approver and a mandatory postmortem within 48 h | author's synthesis; no single source |
| <a id="k3"></a>K3 | [U30](07-rules.md#u30) (tolerate variation) seems to conflict with [C5](07-rules.md#c5) (reject bad input) | tolerate variation within the specification ([U30](07-rules.md#u30)); reject and report input outside the specification ([C5](07-rules.md#c5)) | both rules aim at stable behavior; they differ only in where the boundary lies | accept any valid timestamp format the schema allows; reject a timestamp field that is missing | author's synthesis from Taguchi and RFC 9413 |
| <a id="k4"></a>K4 | preserving evidence ([U23](07-rules.md#u23)) would delay recovery beyond the time until harm ([U35](07-rules.md#u35)) | capture evidence automatically and fast; if harm is imminent, restore service first and keep whatever the automation captured | both the evidence and the service matter, and automation makes the trade-off small | crash dumps and log shipping run before every automatic restart | author's synthesis; no single source |
| <a id="k5"></a>K5 | [U50](07-rules.md#u50) (decide reversible decisions fast) conflicts with [U9](07-rules.md#u9) (change review) and [U8](07-rules.md#u8) (independent checker) | scale the review depth with reversibility and consequence: reversible and low consequence → peer review or self-check; irreversible or high consequence → full review, [R6](07-rules.md#r6) and an external checker | both slowness and unreviewed irreversible harm are failures | a new dashboard: the team decides; deleting historical audit logs: two approvers and a legal review | author's synthesis from the Amazon 2015 letter and the master rule |
| <a id="k6"></a>K6 | [U49](07-rules.md#u49) (leave the method to the people doing the work) conflicts with [U32](07-rules.md#u32) (checklists), [U33](07-rules.md#u33) (critical control points) and [C10](07-rules.md#c10) (qualified procedure) | prescribe the method only at critical control points, in qualified or regulated procedures, and where the rigor matrix requires it; everywhere else give intent and constraints | prescribed methods everywhere stop initiative; free methods at critical points remove proven protections | "use the restore checklist" (prescribed); "improve developer onboarding" (intent only) | author's synthesis from ADP 6-0 and [C10](07-rules.md#c10) |
| <a id="k7"></a>K7 | [U47](07-rules.md#u47) (psychological safety) and [U10](07-rules.md#u10) (blameless analysis) seem to conflict with accountability ([U2](07-rules.md#u2), [U48](07-rules.md#u48), [R9](07-rules.md#r9), legal duties) | treat honest errors and system weaknesses without blame; treat deliberate violations, reckless behavior and fraud through a defined, fair disciplinary procedure | blame for honest errors hides information; no consequence for deliberate violations destroys trust and controls | a wrong command during an incident → blameless postmortem; bypassing the two-person rule on purpose → disciplinary procedure | author's synthesis; no single source |
| <a id="k8"></a>K8 | [U55](07-rules.md#u55) (specific, challenging goals) conflicts with [U51](07-rules.md#u51) (indicators are gamed) and with Deming's rejection of numerical goals | use goals for direction and learning; do not tie pay or ranking mechanically to one indicator; pair every target with a counter-indicator | goals raise performance, but a single rewarded number distorts the work | the team goal "halve build time" has a guard "test coverage does not drop"; bonuses are not calculated from the build time | author's synthesis from Locke & Latham, Campbell and Deming |

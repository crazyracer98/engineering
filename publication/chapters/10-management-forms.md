# 10. Management form of each rule

Each row states what a rule means when the "system" is an organization, a team, a project or a process. The IF / THEN / BECAUSE text in [section 7](07-rules.md) stays valid. Rules [P5](07-rules.md#p5)–[P8](07-rules.md#p8), [P18](07-rules.md#p18)–[P21](07-rules.md#p21), [P35](07-rules.md#p35), [P43](07-rules.md#p43), [P52](07-rules.md#p52), [P53](07-rules.md#p53), [P62](07-rules.md#p62), [P63](07-rules.md#p63), [P69](07-rules.md#p69), [P79](07-rules.md#p79) come from management sources and are already written for organizations; they are not repeated here. "Cross-reference only" in the Sources column means no management source was found; the transfer is the author's judgment (adoption gap, see [section 1](01-how-to-read.md)).
